Flat 2D vector-like engraving, crisp uniform fine lines, no lighting, no perspective, no shadows outside the design. Adapt to a wide horizontal card aspect ratio approximately 1.79:1, filling the image edge to edge with green paper and inset complete border; retain all four corner ornaments with sufficient safe margins. REMOVE ALL TEXT, ALL LETTERS, ALL DIGITS and all typography samples, including the large CASH, FONT, alphabet, number row and punctuation. Continue the delicate pale line pattern seamlessly through those removed areas. There must be absolutely no text or symbols or portrait in the finished blank note; CASH and a changing live amount will be drawn by the application in the centered interior. No heavy floral Victorian frame, no photographic paper texture, no white outside margins. The center must remain pale and calm enough for dark-green live lettering to be legible. Output only the finished blank decorative banknote background.
