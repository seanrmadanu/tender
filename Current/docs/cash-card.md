# Cash note

Cash wallet cards follow the user's replacement reference: pale pistachio paper, fine olive guilloche waves, a repeating oval-loop frame and four spiral curled-leaf corners. The blank background is bundled in `Tender/Assets.xcassets/CashGuillocheNote.imageset`; the separate transparent CASH wordmark is in `CashEngravedTitle.imageset`. Both were generated with the built-in image-generation tool using the supplied reference. The older floral border is no longer displayed. Full prompts are recorded in [generation notes](cash-note-generation.md). Only accounts marked `isCash` receive this design.

The card has no profile photo. CASH is centered above the actual cash balance. Its outlined, chamfered block letters, offset ink and fine horizontal hatching follow the supplied cash-font specimen. Only the word CASH is part of the title asset; the balance stays live and uses heavier rounded numerals with fine paper hatching, centered beneath the title. The existing rolling-number animation and shared three-minute cooldown remain. Changing the cash amount updates the card without creating an extra expense or transaction.

Reveal lifts the card vertically without spinning or showing a reverse face. Hide slides it back into the holder. Card details, account switching and cash balance edits retain their existing behavior. Currency symbols and digits use fixed single-line cells of the same height; the dollar symbol receives the full digit-width allowance. The dark green ink and light note stay legible in both app appearances. No bank artwork is substituted or downloaded.
